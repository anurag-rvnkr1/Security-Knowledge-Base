# Lab: Account Compromise Tabletop

> Category: Incident Response  
> Difficulty: Beginner  
> Estimated Time: 35 minutes  
> Environment: Tabletop discussion; fabricated scenario

## Scenario

At 09:15 UTC, an identity provider records a successful sign-in for a staff test account from an unfamiliar network, followed by an unusual file download. The account owner reports no activity. The records are synthetic; no real identity or tenant is involved.

## Objective

Practice scope, evidence preservation, escalation, and proportionate containment decisions without taking action on a real account.

## Procedure

1. Identify the incident lead, system owner, privacy/legal contact, and decision authority.
2. Establish a timeline and list the evidence needed: authentication method, MFA result, device context, session/token events, file access, and audit changes.
3. Identify gaps and alternative explanations, including travel, VPN use, and approved automation.
4. Propose containment options and note user and business impact. Do not simulate destructive actions.
5. Define recovery checks, communications, and post-incident improvements.

## Expected Results

Produce a scoped evidence checklist, decision log, escalation plan, and follow-up actions. Do not label the event confirmed compromise based only on the scenario.

## Lessons Learned

Record who can authorize containment, where evidence is preserved, and how identity, endpoint, and cloud logs will be correlated. See [Incident Response](../../Incident-Response/).
