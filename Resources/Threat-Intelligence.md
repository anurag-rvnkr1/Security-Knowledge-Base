# Threat Intelligence Resources

| Resource | Type | Use |
|---|---|---|
| [MITRE ATT&CK](https://attack.mitre.org/) | Behavior knowledge base | Tactics, techniques, and defensive context |
| [CISA advisories](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/cybersecurity-advisories) | Government reporting | Validated threat and mitigation information |
| [MISP Project](https://www.misp-project.org/) | Open-source platform | Threat information sharing and correlation |
| [OASIS STIX](https://www.oasis-open.org/standard/stix/) | Standard | Structured threat information |
| [OASIS TAXII](https://www.oasis-open.org/standard/taxii/) | Standard | Exchange protocol for cyber threat information |
| [FIRST TLP](https://www.first.org/tlp/) | Sharing convention | Communicating information handling restrictions |

Indicators are time-sensitive and context-dependent; assess provenance, confidence, and expiry before blocking.
